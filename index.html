High standard. Easy to talk to.

Some instruction hides in jargon. Some of it stays friendly and never gets specific. We are neither. The work is technical. The conversation is open. You should leave knowing what changed, why it changed, and what to practice next.

Accomplished players

Fellers

You run something, or you competed at a high level and you are done being casual about the one game you still play. You do not need the basics. You need the next gain, found on purpose.

We build the plan around the strokes you are actually giving away. Full swing, wedges, putting, and the clubs in the bag. Indoor bays when the numbers matter. The course when it is time to see if the change holds up.

For CEOs, operators, and former athletes with a real game and a real calendar.

Competitive students

Fearless Fellers

Middle school through college. This is not a junior clinic, and it is not a casual lesson between seasons. Fearless Fellers is for students who intend to compete — high school, college, and the players who mean to go on from there.

Same coaches. Same technology. Same directness. What changes is the plan: practice that fits a school week, numbers a player can understand, and an honest read on where the game stands. Families are in the conversation. The player owns the work.

Fearless, here, means honest ambition. We want the student who is driven, and we will match that drive with a clear plan.

For serious competitive students, middle school through college.

How the golf gets better

Measure it. Fit it.
Teach it. Say it.

01 See it

Indoor simulators and launch data. Ball speed, spin, start line, strike. We stop arguing with a feeling and look at the shot.

02 Fit it

Brad is one of the best club fitters in the country. A better motion in the wrong club is still unfinished. Fitting sits inside the coaching.

03 Teach it

Full swing, short game, and putting, with specialists on the staff. The green gets the same attention as the tee.

04 Say it

You leave with the plan in words you can repeat. Open communication is not a courtesy. It is how the work sticks.
